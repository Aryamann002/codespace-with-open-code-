# Design System for Cafe o Late

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Cafe o Late is an artisanal, warm, and sophisticated modern café experience. It embraces rich coffee tones, warm creams, roasted espresso, and calming emerald/olive undertones. The canvas alternates between warm cream (`#f9f6f0`), ceramic off-white (`#edebe9`), and deep roasted espresso green/black (`#1E3932` / `#1b261d`).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Color palette: Deep Espresso (`#1b261d` / `#1E3932`), Rich Latte Amber/Caramel (`#cba258` / `#b37d34`), Warm Cream Canvas (`#f9f6f0`), Ceramic Card (`#ffffff` / `#edebe9`), Warm Muted Text (`rgba(27,38,29,0.88)`).
- Typography: Sophisticated serif headers (Playfair Display / Lander Tall style) paired with clean geometric humanist sans-serif (Inter / SoDoSans style) for body and UI.
- Geometry & Radii: 50px full-pill buttons with micro-interaction hover and press effects (`scale(0.96)`), 16px soft rounded cards.
- Elevation: Subtle, layered warm ambient shadows (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27,38,29,0.08)`).
- Atmosphere: Inviting café ambiance with subtle steam/ambient glow motion, aroma badges, and interactive category filtering for menu specialties.
